Answer the ratio-based question directly before discussing broader liquidity. A quick ratio below 1 means the calculated quick assets do not fully cover current liabilities; state that limitation clearly. Additional credit facilities, cash-generation capacity, or management commentary may provide context, but should not reverse the conclusion about coverage based on the requested ratio alone.

Show the quick assets and current liabilities used in the calculation, identify their reporting date, and cite the source so the reader can verify the ratio.
