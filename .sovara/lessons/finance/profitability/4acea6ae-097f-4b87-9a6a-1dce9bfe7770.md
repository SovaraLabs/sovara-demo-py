Use reported operating margin as the primary basis. Identify its year-over-year change and explain the drivers from the reported-results discussion, including gross-margin changes and material operating charges. Do not substitute an adjusted-margin bridge that excludes those charges. Clearly distinguish any supplemental adjusted figures. Express differences between percentage margins in percentage points.
