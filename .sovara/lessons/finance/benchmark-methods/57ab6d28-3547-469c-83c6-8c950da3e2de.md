Assess capital intensity using relevant metrics supported by the supplied source figures, such as CAPEX/revenue, net PP&E/total assets, and return on assets. Calculate only metrics for which the required inputs are available, and explain how each metric supports the classification. Do not substitute differently defined ratios without labeling them, and do not invent industry thresholds absent from the supplied evidence.
