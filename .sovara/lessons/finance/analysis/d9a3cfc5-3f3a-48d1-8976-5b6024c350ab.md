  the remaining total.

  breakdowns into qualitative terms such as majority, dominant, substantial, or largest.

Calculate and reconcile each cited component’s share of the total before describing the composition. Use “majority” only above 50%, and use “largest category” only after comparing all relevant categories. Do not call a category dominant merely because it is substantial. If the cited components remain a minority of the total, describe them as substantial rather than dominant unless comparison with the other categories supports dominance. Do not infer a business classification from a minority share alone.
